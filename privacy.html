Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Kryvasis ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request through our booking form, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Work email address
  • Phone number
  • Service interest and optional description

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your consultation request
  • Assess service suitability
  • Communicate regarding potential engagements
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Protection

Your data is stored in encrypted databases hosted by Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant infrastructure provider. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Engagement-related data is subject to NDA terms as agreed per engagement.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for database hosting and form data processing. Their privacy policy governs the handling of data on their infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us through the details provided on our contact page.

Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our consultation form.
